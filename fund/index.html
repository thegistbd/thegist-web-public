Origin
Politics does not operate only through laws, police, or elections. It operates through language, framing, emphasis, and omission. What is repeated becomes common sense. What is softened becomes acceptable. What is excluded becomes invisible.

July '24 proved that ordinary Bangladeshis can take control of the language, framing, emphasis and omission. The Gist was born to hold that space open. Not for politicians. Not for domesticated journalists. For you, me, and the regular Bangladeshis, like us and around us — home and abroad. Registered in the UK and run with Bangladeshis on the ground, the Gist operates at the sweet spot of collaboration between the diaspora and the mainland.
